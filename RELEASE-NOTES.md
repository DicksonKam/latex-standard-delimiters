0.4.2 fixes severe slowdown in dense callouts. Source mapping now renders one marked Markdown template per section instead of repeating the entire section for every equation. A 250-equation callout dropped from approximately 10 seconds in Live Preview and 20 seconds in Reading View to about 0.6 seconds in both views on the tested machine. Source remains unchanged.

Verification now includes 31 unit tests with 7,000 deterministic generated inputs and 1,173 actual-Obsidian assertions across 21 runtime/stress suites. New tests cover 40 adversarial cases repeated in both views, nested callouts/titles, identical equations and literal lookalikes, tables, Markdown exclusions, 30 lifecycle races, 1,000 edit dispatches and dense callouts. Current hash-bound stress evidence is required for packaging. See STRESS-TESTING.md and VERIFICATION.md.
